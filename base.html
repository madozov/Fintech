Student Org Compliance & Accounting Standards

Master the UT Knoxville 3-Balance system to prevent overdrafts, enforce tolerance rules, and ensure smooth executive officer handoffs.

1. The 3-Balance Accounting Standard

Traditional bank accounts fail student organizations because checks take weeks to clear at the Student Union. The Shadow Ledger solves this by tracking three concurrent balances:

  • • True Bank Balance: Real cash verified on official Student Union bank statements.
  • • Committed Funds: Sum of all approved estimates locked for future purchases.
  • • Available Balance: True Working Cash = Bank Balance - Committed Funds.

2. Officer Role Privacy Controls

To streamline operations and safeguard financial privacy, Officers view only their own active request queue and personal reimbursement status. Executive balances and bank ledgers are restricted to Treasurers and Presidents.

Officer Permissions:

Officers can submit estimated approval requests, upload reimbursement requests with receipts, track personal payout history, and access training materials.

3. Customizable Overage Tolerance Rules

Officers often encounter small unexpected costs (sales tax, price variations) during actual purchases.

Capped Reimbursement Engine:

If an estimate is approved for $100 and the configurable tolerance cap is 10%, the system automatically caps the max reimbursement at $110, protecting the org budget from unapproved overspending.

4. Executive Transfer Safety Protocol

Prevents unauthorized lockouts during officer handoffs. Incoming Presidents or Treasurers must be invited via official NetID email and accept transfer terms before executive rights switch.
